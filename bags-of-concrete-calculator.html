How many bags of concrete do I need?

One cubic yard (27 ft³) takes 45 bags of 80 lb, 60 bags of 60 lb or 90 bags of 40 lb.

Bag yields

BagYieldBags per cubic yard
80 lb0.60 ft³45
60 lb0.45 ft³60
40 lb0.30 ft³90

Example: 12 × 12 ft patio, 4 in thick

  1. 12 × 12 × (4 ÷ 12) = 48 ft³.
  2. Add 10% waste: 52.8 ft³ (1.96 yd³).
  3. 80 lb bags: 52.8 ÷ 0.60 = 88. 60 lb bags: 118. 40 lb bags: 176.

At this size, mixing 88 bags by hand is a long day. Many contractors compare the bag price against a ready-mix delivery once a pour gets close to a cubic yard; the full calculator below does that comparison for you, including short-load fees.
