How it works

Volume = length × width × thickness, plus a waste allowance. In imperial units the thickness is converted from inches to feet and the result from cubic feet to cubic yards (27 ft³ = 1 yd³). Bags are rounded up, because you can't buy part of a bag.

A 5–10% waste allowance is normal for slabs; use the high end if the ground is uneven. Worked examples: how much concrete for a 10x10 slab and how many bags of concrete you need.
