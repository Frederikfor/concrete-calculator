How much concrete for a 10x10 slab?

A 10 × 10 ft slab 4 inches thick takes 1.23 cubic yards of concrete. With a 10% waste allowance, plan for 1.36 yd³, which is 62 bags of 80 lb.

Step by step

  1. Convert the thickness to feet: 4 in ÷ 12 = 0.333 ft.
  2. Multiply: 10 × 10 × 0.333 = 33.3 ft³.
  3. Convert to cubic yards: 33.3 ÷ 27 = 1.23 yd³.
  4. Add 10% waste: 1.23 × 1.10 = 1.36 yd³ (36.7 ft³).
  5. Bags: 36.7 ÷ 0.60 ft³ per 80 lb bag = 61.1, rounded up to 62.
